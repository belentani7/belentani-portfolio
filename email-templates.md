## General Tips

- **Subject lines:** Keep under 50 characters when possible. Curiosity > formality.
- **Personalization:** Always research the recipient. Reference something specific they've built, posted, or said.
- **CTA:** Every email needs a single clear next step (call, demo, reply).
- **Brevity:** Recruiters skim. Use bullet points and tables over paragraphs.
- **Follow-up cadence:** Day 0 (application), Day 5–7 (follow-up), Day 14 (final nudge).
- **Attachments:** Never attach large files. Link to portfolio, GitHub, or live demos instead.
